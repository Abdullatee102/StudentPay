StudentPay Escrow White Paper

Trustless Peer-to-Peer Student Micro-Services Escrow on Bohr Testnet

Contract Address: 0x7591428059DcAD6De8D51177080959F8B347603D · Chain ID: 968

1. The Problem

Students frequently exchange services with peers for tutoring, design, software development, video editing, essay proofreading, and campus event organization. These informal agreements carry severe risks:

  • Upfront Payment Risk: Buyers pay upfront and sellers ghost or deliver substandard work.
  • Post-Delivery Non-Payment: Sellers deliver finished assets, but buyers refuse to pay.
  • Work-Stealing Vulnerability: Digital previews are often stolen or copied prior to payment release.
  • Centralized Intermediary Overhead: Traditional payment apps charge 10%-20% fees and rely on server databases vulnerable to outages and censorship.

2. The Opportunity

Higher education campuses host vibrant micro-economies. A decentralized, zero-commission escrow platform enables students to monetize skills safely without trusting unknown peers or relying on traditional banking infrastructure.

3. The StudentPay Solution

StudentPay eliminates counterparty risk using non-custodial smart contracts, cryptographic commitments, and dual-level deliverable access control:

Zero Centralized Backend
No Express server, PostgreSQL, or Supabase. The smart contract on Bohr Testnet handles all state and business logic.
Blinded Proof Commitments
Sellers commit a cryptographic hash (keccak256) of their deliverable before the deadline to prevent late work and theft.
Protected Preview Inspection
Buyers inspect watermarked previews (STUDENTPAY • PREVIEW ONLY) to verify quality while usable originals remain locked.
Anti-Ghosting Safeguard
Sellers claim automatic 100% payouts if the 48-hour review window expires without buyer dispute.

4. How StudentPay Works (5-Step Core Flow)

1. CREATE DEAL ──────► 2. SELLER SUBMITS ──────► 3. BUYER INSPECTS ──────► 4. ACCEPT / DISPUTE ──────► 5. DELIVERABLE UNLOCKS (Buyer locks BOT) (Commitment Hash) (Protected Preview) (Instant Payout/Refund) (Original Asset Active)

5. Core Features

  • Blinded Proof Commitment: Computes keccak256 hashes of deliverables on-chain before deadlines.
  • Dual-Level Protected Access: Generates watermarked previews for images, video, audio, code, and web apps while locking original files.
  • 48-Hour Review Window: Provides buyers 48 hours to inspect work or flag disputes before auto-release triggers.
  • Anti-Ghosting Payout: Guarantees seller payment if buyers become unresponsive post-delivery.
  • Deadline Refund Protection: Grants buyers immediate 100% refunds if sellers miss submission deadlines.

8. Security & Trust Mechanisms

  • Non-Custodial Escrow: Contract holds funds; no owner admin key or backdoor exists.
  • Reentrancy Guards: State-locked execution for all payment transfers.
  • Watermark Isolation: Prevents raw file copying or theft prior to payment release.
